# Product truth review: zimmy.art vs the founder's sheet

Reviewer role: PRODUCT TRUTH. The source of truth is `sheet.md`, with the Strategy tab first, then Product Vision, then Problem statements. Page text comes from `sections.json`, and I checked it against the desktop screenshots (hero, ladder, creators).

**Verdict in one line:** the page has become an influencer-ops and agency-replacement pitch aimed only at brands. The sheet describes something else: a **research engine for predictable virality**. For creators it delivers a **content calendar**. For brands it delivers **AI UGC on autopilot**, and the winners move up a chain to UGC and then to ads. The creators get served first.

---

## 1. What Zimmy is, in one sentence

> **Zimmy delivers predictable content outcomes through extensive AI research. It finds the virality patterns in a niche, per geo, and a human in the loop decodes why they worked. For creators it turns this into a content calendar that grows followers and views. For brands it runs AI UGC on autopilot and converts the winners into UGC and then into ads.**

Sheet anchors:
- "Baseline - Predictable outcomes via extensive AI research" / "AI Research will be long term moat"
- Creator P0: "Views & Audience Growth via Predictable Content Outcomes"
- Brand P0: "Campaign Success via Predictable Campaign Design"
- Brands: "AI UGC on Auto-pilot" → "Convert successful AI UGC to UGC" → "Convert successful UGC to Ads"
- Creators: "Content Calendar for Creators", with success criterion "Virality" and goal "Follower / Views Growth"
- Steps 2 and 3: "Research for virality Patterns in that Niche … per Geo and overall EU/English market/ India", and "Decode 'Why' behind the virality … Human Intervention - HITL"

**Hero promise derived from it:**

> **Go viral on purpose.**
> Sub-line: *Zimmy researches what's going viral in your niche and market, then turns it into a content calendar for creators and AI UGC on autopilot for brands.*

Alternative headline if "predictable" must carry the weight: **"Virality, made predictable."**

The current hero, "A recipe for viral content", is close in spirit. Its body copy, though, ends on "Not guaranteed. Just far less guessing." That is defensive framing. The sheet's own word is *predictable*, so lead with it. Keep the honesty in the FAQ.

## 3. Missing list (in the sheet but not said clearly on the page)

1. **"AI UGC on autopilot."** The sheet names this as the first brand objective. The page never uses the words "AI UGC" or "autopilot". "UGC" appears only in "UGC agencies", where it's used against competitors. **This is the biggest gap.**
2. **The conversion chain, AI UGC → UGC → Ads**, in the sheet's own words. The ladder sort of shows it ("AI videos → real creators → ads") but frames it as "Test with AI. Scale with creators.", which puts the weight on creators. The Vision line "AI UGC > UGC > Ads > Influencer Marketing" should be stated literally, as three steps where each conversion is earned by results.
3. **Virality as the success criterion, with reach, conversion, low CPM and scale as goals.** "Viral" appears in the hero only. "Campaign Reach, Conversion" and "Low CPM, high scalability" appear nowhere. The closest line is "cost per result holds as spend grows". Brands need to hear **low CPM** and **scale** explicitly.
4. **Predictability as the core promise.** The base thought is "Predictable outcomes via extensive AI research" and the moat is AI research. The page says "predictable" once, inside an FAQ answer. Its tone is "Not guaranteed. Just far less guessing." Flip it.
5. **Creators' content calendar for follower and views growth.** It exists, but it sits 10th on the page, is labelled "Coming soon" and says "grow your views" only. The sheet says "**Follower / Views Growth**" and the Vision tab calls it a "*Creator Growth Plan". Followers are missing.
6. **Creators come first (GTM).** There's no creator CTA in the hero or navbar. Navbar "Join as a creator" → creator.zimmy.art fixes the navbar only. The hero and the closing CTA need the same treatment.
7. **The full Step 0–8 workflow.** The page has five stages. These steps are missing or unclear:
   - **Step 0 "Define Marketing AHA Moment"**: partly covered ("the moment someone watching 'gets it'"), but "AHA moment" is a good brand hook to use by name.
   - **Step 1 "Define Category / Content Niche"**: implied only.
   - **Step 5 "Create AI UGC Video"** and **Step 6 "Post AI UGC videos across Platforms"** are merged into "Test". The page never says Zimmy creates and posts AI UGC as one flow.
   - **Step 7 "Post Live Analytics"** is spread across the platforms and tech sections instead of sitting as one step.
8. **"Global narrative with local relevance."** Geo research is on the page, but this framing, which is the sheet's segment thesis, isn't. It's a strong one-liner for the market section.
9. **"Full Funnel View with insights."** No section says the brand sees one funnel from AI UGC to UGC to ads with insights at each stage. The page shows fragments (clicks, views, "sales next").

## 4. Audience order: brands, creators or a split?

**Recommendation: split clearly at the hero, with creators first in order and brands first in depth.**

- **The hero is one shared promise ("Go viral on purpose") with two equal buttons.** Put **"I'm a creator"** (→ creator.zimmy.art) on the left and **"I'm a brand"** (→ book a demo) on the right. One research engine serves both stakeholders, which is exactly how the sheet's value table is built.
- **Section 2 is the creator block**: the content calendar for follower and views growth, built on what's going viral in your niche, with brand deals as a secondary benefit. Keep it short, at one screen.
- **Section 3 onwards is the brand story**: AI UGC on autopilot → UGC → ads, the Step 0–8 loop, geo research and HITL. It's longer because the brand product is deeper.

Reasoning:
1. **The GTM says creators first.** "First | Creator first strategy … Direct Reach - outs to Micro Creators … Build Proof of Concept of follower / views Growth." "Third | Brands for UGC Programs." Micro creators who land on a page that only says "Book a demo", and that calls them "you too" in section 10, will bounce. Outreach to creators is the current motion, so the page has to convert them.
2. **Don't go creator-only.** The Strategy table lists Brands first, with three of its four objectives. Brands are where the money and the conversion chain sit, and a UGC partner (GTM step two) will judge Zimmy on the brand chain too. A creator-only page would hide the core product.
3. **The new navbar button "Join as a creator" → creator.zimmy.art** works with this setup. Use the same label and link in the hero and the final CTA, and replace "Join the creator list" so there's one creator path.
4. **Status honesty:** the creator calendar is labelled "Coming soon". A creator-first hero can still lead with it as a waitlist ("Get your growth calendar"). That's fine for an acquisition phase. Don't invent results.
