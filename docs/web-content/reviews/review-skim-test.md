# Five-second skim test: zimmy.art

Method: I looked at every screenshot, desktop (23) first and then mobile (37). For each one I gave about five seconds to what a skimmer's eye lands on: big type, visuals, badges and buttons. Paragraphs are treated as unread. I ran the test as three personas and then compared what each one took away against the founder's sheet (`sheet.md`).

What the sheet says Zimmy is: **predictable content outcomes from deep AI research.** For brands, that means research leads to AI UGC tests, the winners go to real creators, and the creator winners become ads. For creators, the top priority (P0) is **views and audience growth** through a content calendar, and brand deals come second. The go-to-market plan is **creator-first**: reach out to micro creators and prove follower growth before selling to brands.

## Big-type elements that confuse

**Jargon**
- **"Outlier"** appears in the hero chip ("Outlier · 6.2× usual"), the "Find outliers" button, "Outlier board", "Outliers, not raw views." and "Find and decode outlier videos". The FAQ has to ask "What is an outlier?", which shows the word doesn't explain itself. Better options: "breakout videos" or "videos beating their usual".
- **"The ladder"** (navbar). No heading on the page says "ladder", so a click lands on "Test with AI. Scale with creators." and the skimmer can't connect the two.
- **"Decode"**, **"Read and decide"**, **"read on its own"**. "Read" is used to mean "analyse", which skimmers don't parse that way.
- **"Operator"** is fine for investors and opaque for creators.
- **"$30M+ in yearly campaign funnels automated end-to-end"**: a big number with an unclear unit.

**Headings that only work with their paragraph (red flags for a headings-only page)**
- **"What goes into the recipe."** Ingredients of what? Without the paragraph it says nothing.
- **"Every platform, read on its own."** Needs "a weak TikTok doesn't cancel a strong Reel" to make sense.
- **"A loop, not a lucky guess."** Borderline: abstract, but rescued by the step list.
- **"Built for teams that need content to work."** Generic. Every competitor could write it.
- **"Change one thing at a time."** Card heading that needs its paragraph.
- **"Cost per test goes up at each step. So does trust."** Clever, but needs a second read.
- **"Everything in between."** Only works next to "Decide. Approve."; fine on desktop, but on mobile the two cards are a scroll apart.

**Big type that sends the wrong signal**
- **"Winning videos will appear here."** The largest type in the demo is an empty state.
- **"Find what's working for people building habits."** A sample prompt styled like a section heading. Skimmers think the page has switched topic.
- **"Illustrative videos, not real clients."** Honest, but read as "no clients". "Example videos" would say the same thing.
- **"Coming soon" on Step 01 ("AI videos find the winner")**, the step the hero promises ("tests that recipe with AI videos").
- **The "$1,400 → $1,100 → Deal" chat card**, which reads as rate-haggling to creators.

**Headings that work standalone (keep these)**
"Research first. Then make videos." / "Test with AI. Scale with creators." / "AI does the work. People check the calls." / "Tools stop at ideas. Agencies start with a guess." / "Making videos? Zimmy works for you too." / "Find your recipe. Then scale it." / "Decide. Approve." / "Videos that beat their own average." / "Paid brand deals."

**Mobile-specific**
- **The comparison table is cut off.** The viewport shows only "Capability" and "Research tools" (mobile-21/22). The Zimmy column, the one with all the ticks, is off-screen to the right. A mobile skimmer sees a column of ✓ / – / ✗ for *competitors* and never sees the punchline.
- The floating "Talk to the founder" pill covers content in almost every mobile frame, for example the compare rows, the solution text, and "The first three tests we'd run" in the CTA.
- The Tech carousel shows one card plus a sliver, so the swipe affordance is weak and three of the four cards go unseen.

## The 5 highest-impact fixes (ranked)

1. **Make the hero's big type say what Zimmy is and who it's for, and give creators a path from the first screen.**
   Keep "A recipe for viral content." if loved, but put a large subline under it that a skimmer will read, built from the footer sentence: *"We find what's already working, test it with AI videos, then scale winners with real creators and ads."* Use the phrase "AI UGC" somewhere visible. Next to "Book a demo", add a second button: **"I'm a creator →"**. Today the hero speaks to brands only, which contradicts the creator-first go-to-market.

2. **Fix the mobile comparison table.**
   Put the Zimmy column first, directly after the capability, or turn the table into three stacked cards (Research tools / **Zimmy** / Agencies). Right now mobile skimmers see only the competitor column, so the section works against Zimmy.

3. **Remove the trust-killers a skimmer sees in big type.**
   - Change "Illustrative videos, not real clients" to "Example videos".
   - Relabel Step 01 from "Coming soon" to "Early access", or say what is live today.
   - Replace the rate-haggling chat card with a neutral "Brief sent → Accepted" or "Paid".
   - Give "$30M+" a plain label, for example "$30M+ in campaign spend run by the founder".
   - Add any proof at all: a pilot number, a logo, or one real before-and-after.

4. **Rewrite the headings that need their paragraph, and drop the jargon.**
   - "Outlier" becomes "breakout video" in all big type, buttons and chips.
   - Nav "The ladder" becomes "AI → Creators → Ads", or simply "How we scale".
   - "What goes into the recipe." becomes "What you get: what worked, why, and a brief to shoot."
   - "Every platform, read on its own." becomes "Judged per platform: TikTok, Reels, Shorts."
   - "Built for teams that need content to work." becomes "For apps, DTC brands and new-market launches."
   - Make the demo's resting state show a filled board, not "Winning videos will appear here."

5. **Cut the page by about a third so the strong sections and the creator section rise.**
   - Merge "What goes into the recipe" and "AI does the work" into one section, since they show the same four cards.
   - Delete the three-icon row under it and the duplicate hero strip, so "human checks" appears once or twice instead of five times.
   - Drop Platforms or fold it into the How loop.
   - Remove the empty coloured blocks in Solutions.
   - Move the Creators section up, and lead it with the sheet's P0, **growing your views**, not brand deals.

   This also helps the investor reach the founder and compare sections before they drop off.
