## The three rules behind every cut

1. **One idea, one place.** The page said "a person checks the calls" at least 6 times, "your real product, never invented" at least 3 times, and "research per market" at least 4 times. Each now appears once. The hero trust strip carries all three.
2. **The visual is the explanation.** Wherever a card had a mock-up (score chips, hook table, timeline, calendar, ladder colours), the paragraph under the title went. Title + chip + visual.
3. **No right-hand paragraphs.** Every section had a 25-40 word grey paragraph beside the headline. None of them survive except where they became a single line of 12 words or fewer.

Kept on purpose: every "Illustrative example(s)" label, every Live / Coming soon / Done by our team chip, the compare-table footnote (legal cover), and the "Timings are typical" caveat (we state day counts).

Nothing here is new. Every line below is either existing page text cut down or a merge of two existing lines. No features or numbers are added.

## Notes for whoever builds it

- **Order after cuts:** Hero → Research → What goes into the recipe → Ladder → How it works → Platforms → Compare → Creators → Founder → FAQ → CTA → Footer. Tech and Solutions are gone, so the page loses about three full screens of scroll.
- **Nav:** "Why Zimmy" should point at the compare table. Nothing in the nav pointed at Solutions or Tech, so no links break.
- **Hero cards:** the chips become the main text on each card, so give them a little more weight now that the captions are gone.
- **How it works:** with no step descriptions, the expanding row only needs the progress bar. Consider making the five rows static.
- **What grid:** the Experiment board card loses its list. Either keep the three numbered rows as unlabeled visual bars or let the card sit shorter.
- **If the founder wants more back,** restore in this order: (1) the platforms reel with chips only, about 30 words; (2) the ladder's three "Move up when" lines, about 30 words; (3) "Do I stay in control?" in the FAQ, 13 words.
