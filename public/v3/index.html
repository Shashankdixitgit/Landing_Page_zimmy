A loop,
not a lucky guess.

Every round teaches the next one. You make the calls at each checkpoint. Zimmy does the work in between.

  1. Your product, your audience, and the moment someone watching “gets it”. Then the niche and market to study.

  2. Zimmy finds outlier videos in that niche, market by market.

  3. Why each video worked, checked by a strategist. Then a ranked board of what to test.

  4. AI videos and creator videos go out on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

  5. Results per platform. Double down, remake with real creators, or drop it, within your budget.
